# Light Titan Labs redesign

## Direction
Rework the storefront around the supplied DarkMatter//BIO screenshots while preserving all existing shopping, account, checkout, order, and administration features. The result will be light, bright, technical, and premium rather than predominantly black.

## What will change
- Replace the dark palette with white and cool silver surfaces, pale blue-grey sections, deep navy text, electric-blue highlights, and restrained metallic-gold details sampled from the Titan logo.
- Rebuild the header in the reference style: slim branded navigation, generous spacing, precise outlined controls, and a subtle logo-free Titan background image.
- Recompose the home page with a spacious light hero, strong technical headline typography, restrained actions, and clean specification bands.
- Restyle the catalogue, product cards, product pages, forms, account pages, checkout, and admin surfaces so they share one consistent light visual system.
- Rebuild the footer as a large logo-free visual section using Titan-themed metallic and architectural imagery, followed by clean navigation and research-use information.
- Preserve the current editable page content, section ordering, product filters, cart, authentication, and admin controls.

## Custom imagery
Create two original, logo-free Titan visuals:
- Header: pale silver architectural metal with fine blue and gold edge details.
- Footer: a deeper but still luminous composition of brushed titanium, abstract Spartan geometry, and subtle Australian star references.

Neither image will contain words, emblems, or logos.

## Typography and finish
Use wide, engineered display lettering inspired by the logo for headings and navigation, paired with a highly readable modern body typeface. Keep text spacing, borders, shadows, and metallic treatments precise rather than decorative.

## Verification
Check the home page, catalogue, a product page, login, account, checkout, and admin views on desktop and mobile. Confirm navigation, search, category filters, cart, editable content, text wrapping, and contrast remain clear.
